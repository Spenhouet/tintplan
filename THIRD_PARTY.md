# Third-party data

The pen colors in `src/lib/pens/data/` are converted by `scripts/import-pens.ts` from these projects. Both are MIT licensed; their notices are reproduced below.

| Palettes                                                                 | Source                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Copic Sketch, Ciao, Classic                                              | [scottgfhong310/copic-color](https://github.com/scottgfhong310/copic-color)                 |
| Faber-Castell Polychromos, Albrecht Dürer, Goldfaber, Black Edition      | [scottgfhong310/faber-castell-color](https://github.com/scottgfhong310/faber-castell-color) |
| Caran d'Ache Luminance, Pablo, Supracolor, Museum Aquarelle, Neocolor II | [scottgfhong310/caran-dache-color](https://github.com/scottgfhong310/caran-dache-color)     |
| Finecolour markers                                                       | [scottgfhong310/finecolour-color](https://github.com/scottgfhong310/finecolour-color)       |
| Ohuhu Honolulu, Oahu, Kaala; DecoTime                                    | [dferrandizmont/ink-lab](https://github.com/dferrandizmont/ink-lab)                         |

The hex values were sampled from the makers' printed or PDF color charts. They are screen approximations, not official specifications. Brand and product names are trademarks of their owners; Tintplan is not affiliated with any of them.
